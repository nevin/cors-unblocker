Welcome to CORS Unblocker

  1. Open the page you're developing, such as localhost:3000, and click the CORS Unblocker icon.
  2. Click Trust. Calls that page makes to other sites get relaxed CORS headers. Other websites are not affected.
  3. A red number on the icon means calls on that tab were blocked by CORS. Open the popup and click Fix.

Tip: pin the icon to your toolbar from the extensions menu so its state is always visible. Overrides start switched off.

Rules

Unblock single API calls, or trust a whole page you're developing. Skip rules always keep a URL's original headers.

Discovered requests

URLs pages called recently while overrides were on. Unblock relaxes just that call from the page that made it; Skip keeps its original headers.

    Behavior

    Turn overrides on or off, and choose which pages they apply to.

    Scope

    Global mode relaxes CORS headers for every website, not just your active rules. Switch back to trusted pages only when you are done debugging.
