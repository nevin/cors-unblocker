Welcome to CORS Unblocker

  1. Open the page you're developing, such as localhost:3000, and click the CORS Unblocker icon.
  2. Click Trust. Calls that page makes to other sites get relaxed CORS headers. Other websites are not affected.
  3. A red number on the icon means calls on that tab were blocked by CORS. Open the popup and click Fix.

Tip: pin the icon to your toolbar from the extensions menu so its state is always visible. Overrides start switched off.

Rules

Trust the pages you're developing: calls they make get relaxed CORS headers. Skip rules keep a URL's original headers.

Hosts, full URLs, wildcard subdomains (*.example.dev) and paths ending in /* all work.
